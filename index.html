Press Scan Markets to pull live order books and rank the best 3-leg cycles across exchanges.
How this works — every leg multiplies your balance by the best bid (selling) or 1/ask (buying) available on that exchange's public order book, minus your taker fee. A positive % means the round trip A→B→C→A returns more than you started with, before slippage. All three legs of one cycle always execute on the same exchange — arbitrage never mixes order books mid-cycle.
Read before trading — this is an informational scan of top-of-book prices, not a guarantee, and it does not manufacture profit that isn't really there. Real fills depend on order book depth, execution speed, and simultaneous availability of all three legs — thin books and latency routinely erase small or negative edges either way. This is not financial advice.
