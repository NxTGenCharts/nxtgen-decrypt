Security & API

Your exchange access stays under your control.

Create keys with the minimum permissions you need. Never enable withdrawals, and use your exchange's IP allow-list where available. Browser storage is convenient, not a vault, so only connect keys on a device you trust.

Your keys, your call

You enter your own exchange API keys. They are kept in your browser's local storage on your device.

Read-only verification

Connecting an exchange only reads your balance. Verification places no orders and makes no withdrawals.

Trading is opt-in

Orders are only placed after you connect keys with trading permission and explicitly start Autotrade or Futures.

Signed, not stored

Balance and order requests are signed by the app's backend proxy in memory for that single request. Per its documentation, keys are not written to disk, a database or logs.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

NxTGen DeCrypt is a browser-based arbitrage terminal. It loads market data from supported exchanges, scans for triangular and cross-exchange price discrepancies, and shows the numbers behind each opportunity. It also includes an Autotrade & Futures section and utilities such as paper trading.

Arbitrage means looking for the same asset, or a closed loop of assets, priced inconsistently across markets. Any gap has to be large enough to cover trading fees, slippage and latency to be worth acting on, and gaps can close quickly.

A triangular cycle starts and ends in the same currency and passes through three trading pairs on one exchange, for example USDT → BTC → ETH → USDT. If multiplying the three rates, after fees, returns more than you started with, the cycle shows a positive result on paper.

It compares the price of the same pair on different exchanges and highlights the spread between them. Acting on it involves real-world constraints such as balances on each exchange, transfer time, and withdrawal and trading fees.
