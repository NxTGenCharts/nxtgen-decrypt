Account actions
Delete my account…

This permanently removes your account. What happens:

  • Your saved exchange keys are erased from the server. Nothing is changed on the exchanges themselves — delete the API keys there too if you no longer want them to exist.
  • Your bots, their settings and the trade records the server kept are erased. Records already copied into this browser remain until you clear its data.
  • The sign-in itself is removed, so you cannot log back in.

Bots must be stopped first. If any bot is still running, deletion is refused — stop it from Utilities & Tools → Trading Bots (Stop cancels its orders and closes its position), then come back. Nothing is closed for you automatically.
