About

NxTGen DeCrypt is a browser-based arbitrage terminal for traders who want to see the numbers behind every opportunity.

What it is

A single place to scan triangular and cross-exchange price gaps across Bitget, Binance, Bybit, MEXC and Gate.io, with an AI Futures Engine, paper trading and utilities alongside.

Principles

  • Data first. Every result shows the calculation and the assumptions behind it.
  • You stay in control. You decide when to trade and which permissions your keys have.
  • No promises. The terminal surfaces estimates, not guarantees.
